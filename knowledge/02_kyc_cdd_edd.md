# Know Your Customer (KYC), Customer Due Diligence (CDD), and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

## KYC Baseline
Every customer relationship requires identity verification (name, address, date of birth, and identification number) at onboarding, consistent with the Bank's Customer Identification Program (CIP). This baseline applies regardless of product, channel, or customer segment.

## Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
CDD extends KYC by requiring the Bank to understand the nature and purpose of the customer relationship, in order to develop a customer risk profile. CDD risk factors include:
- Customer type (individual, small business, corporate, trust, non-profit).
- Products and services used (e.g. wire transfers, cross-border payments, cash-intensive services).
- Geographic risk (customers or counterparties located in higher-risk jurisdictions).
- Expected account activity, used later to identify anomalous behavior.

## Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
EDD applies to customers or relationships presenting elevated risk, and requires deeper scrutiny, more frequent review, and senior approval. Triggers for EDD include:
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their close associates or family members.
- Customers domiciled in, or transacting heavily with, high-risk or sanctioned jurisdictions.
- Correspondent banking relationships and other financial institutions as customers.
- Cash-intensive businesses (e.g. money services businesses, casinos, certain retail).
- Unusually complex ownership structures that obscure beneficial ownership.

## Relevance to New Product / Change Assessments
When a proposed change introduces a new customer segment, new geography, or a new onboarding flow, reviewers must determine:
- Whether the existing CDD questionnaire captures the risk factors relevant to the new segment or geography.
- Whether EDD triggers need to be updated to catch newly in-scope customer types.
- Whether beneficial ownership collection requirements are satisfied for any new entity types being onboarded.
- Whether review frequency (periodic KYC refresh cadence) needs to change for the new population.
