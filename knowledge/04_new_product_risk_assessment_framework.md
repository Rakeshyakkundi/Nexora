# New Product / Change Risk Assessment Framework

## When This Framework Applies
Any of the following triggers a mandatory financial crime risk assessment before launch:
- Launching a new product or feature (e.g. instant transfers, new account type, embedded finance offering).
- Onboarding a new third-party vendor or fintech partner that touches customer funds, data, or onboarding.
- Entering a new geography or jurisdiction.
- Opening the product to a new customer segment (e.g. minors, non-resident aliens, small businesses, MSBs).
- Making a material change to an existing process that affects customer onboarding, transaction flow, or monitoring.

## Assessment Structure
A complete risk assessment covers:
1. **Change description** — what is changing, who it affects, and expected volume/scale.
2. **Financial crime risk identification** — money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions, fraud, and bribery/corruption exposure introduced or altered by the change.
3. **Control mapping** — which existing controls (KYC/CDD/EDD, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, SAR filing) already cover the change, and which controls have gaps.
4. **Risk rating** — an overall rating of Low, Medium, or High, based on the severity and likelihood of unmitigated risk.
5. **Recommendation** — Approve, Approve with Conditions, Defer, or Reject, with any conditions clearly stated and owned.

## Risk Rating Heuristics
- **High risk** signals: new cross-border corridors, new cash-handling mechanisms, new correspondent relationships, onboarding of MSBs or other high-risk entity types, or any control gap with no compensating mitigant identified.
- **Medium risk** signals: changes affecting existing higher-risk populations without introducing entirely new risk types, or gaps with a clear, time-bound remediation plan.
- **Low risk** signals: changes with no new customer, geography, or payment-mechanism exposure, and full existing control coverage.

## Committee Decision Outcomes
- **Approve** — no material gap identified.
- **Approve with Conditions** — launch permitted subject to specific, tracked remediation items (e.g. "update EDD thresholds for new corridor within 30 days").
- **Defer** — insufficient information to assess; return to submitter for more detail.
- **Reject** — unmitigated risk exceeds the Bank's risk appetite.
